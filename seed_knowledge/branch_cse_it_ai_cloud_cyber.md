## Where the Real Growth & Demand Is (2026–2035)
Basic manual coding and repetitive IT support are shrinking due to AI automation — **demand has shifted sharply to higher-value roles**:
1. **AI / ML Engineering, GenAI & MLOps:** Building production AI agents, RAG pipelines, model evaluation, fine-tuning, and deploying scalable ML systems. Projected 1M+ AI/tech roles in India. Mid-senior: ~₹18–40+ LPA.
2. **Data Engineering & Analytics:** Companies drown in messy data — Data Engineers (SQL, Python, Spark, Airflow, Snowflake, BigQuery) and BI Analysts (Power BI, Tableau, SQL) are in constant demand across banking, retail, healthcare, manufacturing.
3. **Cloud Computing, DevOps & SRE:** AWS, Azure, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD pipelines. High stability + strong salaries (~₹12–30+ LPA mid-senior).
4. **Cybersecurity & Cloud Security:** Rising cyberattacks + India's DPDP Act compliance = massive shortage of SOC Analysts, Cloud Security Engineers, VAPT/Ethical Hackers, GRC specialists.
5. **Backend & Full-Stack System Architecture:** Engineers who can design scalable, reliable systems using AI coding copilots (Node.js/TypeScript, Python, Java/Spring Boot, Go, React/Next.js + Postgres/Redis).

## Crucial Truth for Working Professionals in IT
- **Skills & portfolio get interviews, BUT a recognized UGC degree (B.Tech CSE / BCA / MCA / M.Tech) is strictly required by HR background verification** at TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, HCL, Cognizant, Capgemini, banks, and global MNCs — AND for H1B/work visas abroad!
- **Pathways:**
  - *12th Pass (Any Stream) working in BPO / Tech Support / Sales / Freelancing:* **Online BCA (3 yrs, ~₹15–25k/sem, NAAC A+/A++ via College Vidya)** → build Python/SQL/Web/Cloud skills alongside → transition to Developer/Data/Cloud role → later **Online MCA** if needed.
  - *Diploma CSE / IT / Any Branch working in IT:* **B.Tech CSE Lateral Entry (3 yrs)** (Sanskriti / Kalinga for private Pan-India; Lingaya's for Govt/PSU in 75km NCR ≥50%) OR **Online BCA** (if wanting 100% online + lower fee).
  - *Non-IT Graduate (B.Com, BA, B.Sc, BBA) wanting Tech/IT Switch:* **Online MCA (2 yrs)** (with bridge subjects where applicable) + practical GitHub portfolio.
  - *B.Tech CSE/IT Working Professional:* **M.Tech CSE / AI-DS (WP)** for deep tech/AI/R&D/teaching OR **Online MBA (IT / Business Analytics / Product Mgmt)** for Tech Manager / Product Manager / VP Engineering track.
