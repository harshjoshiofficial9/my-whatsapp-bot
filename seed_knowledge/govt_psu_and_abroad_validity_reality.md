## 1. Govt / PSU Employees Reality
- **JE (Diploma) vs AE / Executive (B.Tech):** Diploma qualifies for JE posts (SSC JE, RRB JE, State PWD/Electricity Boards). Departmental promotion from JE → AE often takes 10–15+ years unless supported by a recognized engineering degree + departmental exam/quota.
- **CRITICAL HONESTY RULE:**
  - For Govt/PSU Working Professional B.Tech/M.Tech, our **ONLY** partner option is **Lingaya's Vidyapeeth** (Deemed UGC Sec 3, Direct AICTE Approved, NAAC A+, ≥50%, within **75 km of Faridabad / Delhi-NCR**).
  - **NEVER guarantee** that any specific PSU (ONGC, NTPC, IOCL, Railways, State Board) will automatically grant promotion or accept a degree without prior approval!
  - **ALWAYS advise honestly:** *"Admission lene se pehle apne department/HR se NOC (No Objection Certificate) aur internal promotion / recruitment rules zaroor confirm kar lein."*
  - Also note: Some central Maharatna PSUs via GATE strictly check full-time regular degree norms — always tell students to check the exact recruitment notification of their target exam/PSU!

## 2. Gulf / Middle East / Abroad (UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Europe, Canada)
- In the **Middle East (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Saudi Arabia, Qatar)**, an experienced Indian Diploma holder often gets stuck on a **Technician / Foreman Visa** (lower salary, limited family visa), whereas a recognized **B.Tech / Bachelor's degree** unlocks **Engineer / Manager Visas** with 1.5x–2.5x higher packages!
- **What matters for abroad:** UGC recognition (`ugc.ac.in`), AIU membership (`aiu.ac.in`), proper apostille/embassy attestation, and WES/credential evaluation rules of the target country. Always verify AIU/country-specific equivalence before promising abroad migration!
