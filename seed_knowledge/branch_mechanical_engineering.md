## Core Subjects
Thermodynamics, Strength of Materials (SOM), Theory of Machines (TOM), Fluid Mechanics, Heat & Mass Transfer, Machine Design, Manufacturing Technology (CNC/Casting/Welding), Industrial Engineering, CAD/CAM/FEA, Refrigeration & HVAC.

## Where the Real Growth & Demand Is (2026–2035 India & Global)
Traditional pure workshop roles have slow growth, BUT **modern Mechanical Engineering is booming in 6 high-growth sectors**:
1. **Electric Vehicles (EV) & Battery Thermal Management:** India's massive shift from ICE to EV (Tata, Mahindra, Ola, Ather, Bosch, Tier-1 auto suppliers) needs Mechanical engineers for battery pack enclosures, thermal cooling design, lightweight chassis, NVH, and drivetrain assembly. Mid-senior range: ~₹12–25+ LPA.
2. **Industrial Automation, Robotics & Industry 4.0:** Smart factories need Mechanical engineers who combine mechanics with pneumatics, hydraulics, robotics kinematics, and basic PLC/IoT understanding.
3. **CAD / CAE / FEA Product Design & R&D:** Global Capability Centers (GCCs) in Pune, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, NCR (Bosch, Eaton, Caterpillar, Airbus, Rolls-Royce, Siemens, L&T) hire B.Tech/M.Tech engineers for structural/thermal simulation — much higher pay & desk-based career than shop-floor!
4. **Aerospace, Defense & Precision Manufacturing:** Make-in-India defense corridors (UP, Tamil Nadu) and private aerospace suppliers (HAL, Tata Advanced Systems, Godrej Aerospace).
5. **HVAC, MEP & Green Energy / Data Center Cooling:** Explosion of AI Data Centers in India (Mumbai, Noida, Hyderabad, Chennai) is driving huge demand for HVAC & thermal design engineers!
6. **Supply Chain, Quality (Six Sigma) & Plant Operations:** High-paying management track in FMCG, Pharma, Auto, and Heavy Manufacturing.

## High-ROI Tools & Skills to Pair With Degree
- *Design/R&D Track:* **SolidWorks, CATIA, Creo, AutoCAD, ANSYS (FEA/CFD), GD&T (ASME Y14.5)**.
- *Manufacturing/Quality Track:* **CNC Programming, Lean Manufacturing, Six Sigma (Green/Black Belt), Kaizen, 7 QC Tools, SAP PM/PP/MM**.
- *Future-Proof Edge:* Basic **Python / MATLAB** for simulation/data or **EV Battery Thermal / BMS basics**.

## Degree Roadmap by Stage
- **Diploma Mechanical + Working:** Do **B.Tech Mechanical Lateral Entry (3 yrs)** (Private Pan-India → Sanskriti or Kalinga; Govt/PSU in 75km NCR ≥50% → Lingaya's) to break the Supervisor/Maintenance ceiling and enter Design / Production Engineer / Assistant Manager roles.
- **B.Tech Mechanical + Working:**
  - Want **R&D, CAD/CAE, Thermal, EV Design, Aerospace, or Teaching?** → **M.Tech (Thermal / Machine Design / Production / CAD-CAM — confirm branch in KB)**.
  - Want **Plant Head, Operations Manager, Supply Chain, Procurement, or faster CTC jump?** → **Online MBA / Executive MBA (Operations / Supply Chain / Project Management)**.
