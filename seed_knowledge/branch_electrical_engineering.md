## Core Subjects
Circuit Theory, Electrical Machines (Transformers, Induction/Synchronous Motors), Power Systems (Generation, Transmission, Distribution), Power Electronics, Control Systems, Switchgear & Protection, High Voltage Engineering, Analog/Digital Electronics, Electrical Measurements & Instrumentation.

## Where the Real Growth & Demand Is (2026–2035)
Electrical Engineering is entering a **golden decade** due to global electrification and decarbonization:
1. **Renewable Energy, Solar, BESS & Green Hydrogen:** India's 500 GW non-fossil energy push (Adani Green, Tata Power, ReNew, NTPC Renewable, Sterling & Wilson) is creating huge demand for Solar PV design, Grid Integration, Substation (33kV–400kV), and Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) engineers. Mid-senior: ~₹10–22+ LPA.
2. **EV Powertrains, Motor Drives & Charging Infrastructure:** Power Electronics engineers working on inverters, DC-DC converters, onboard chargers, and fast-charging station grids. Mid-senior: ~₹12–26+ LPA.
3. **Industrial Automation (PLC / SCADA / DCS / VFD):** Every modern factory (pharma, food, auto, steel, cement, oil & gas) runs on automated panels. High demand in India AND Europe/Gulf!
4. **Smart Grids, Transmission & Data Center Power Systems:** Uninterrupted high-reliability power architecture (UPS, switchgear, backup power) for booming hyperscale data centers.
5. **MEP (Electrical Design for Buildings/Infra):** Lighting, earthing, load calculations, panel design for airports, malls, hospitals, metro stations.

## High-ROI Tools & Skills to Pair With Degree
- *Power & Solar:* **ETAP, PVsyst, AutoCAD Electrical, DIALux, PSS/E**.
- *Automation:* **Siemens TIA Portal, Allen-Bradley / Rockwell PLC, Schneider / ABB SCADA, HMI, VFD, Modbus/Profibus**.
- *EV / Power Electronics:* **MATLAB / Simulink, Embedded C, Power PCB basics**.

## Degree Roadmap by Stage
- **Diploma Electrical (Maintenance / Technician / Panel / JE) + Working:** **B.Tech Electrical Lateral Entry (3 yrs)** breaks the Technician/Electrician-Supervisor ceiling and unlocks Project Engineer, Commissioning Engineer, Solar/Automation Design, and MNC/Gulf roles. (Private Pan-India → Sanskriti/Kalinga; Govt/PSU in 75km NCR ≥50% → Lingaya's.)
- **B.Tech Electrical + Working:**
  - Technical specialization (Power Systems, Power Electronics, Renewable Energy, Control/Automation) → **M.Tech WP**.
  - Project Delivery, Utility Operations, EPC Management, Energy Business → **Online MBA / Executive MBA**.
