Summary: Legal degree paths for Polytechnic Diploma holders who do NOT want to do B.Tech.

## Why a Diploma Holder Might Skip B.Tech
1. Lost interest in core engineering / shop-floor shifts.
2. Wants to switch into **IT/Software/Coding** or **Corporate Management/Sales/Operations**.
3. Budget constraint (~₹45–55k/sem for WP B.Tech feels high; Online UG is ~₹15–25k/sem).
4. Primary goal is **Government Exams** (SSC CGL, UPSC, Banking PO, State PCS) that require *any* UGC-recognized graduation degree.

## Legal Pathways (3-Year Polytechnic Diploma = 10+2 / 12th Equivalent for UG Admissions)
- **Online BCA (3 Yrs, UGC-DEB, ~₹15k–25k/sem):** Best for Diploma holders (even Mechanical/Civil/Electrical) pivoting into Software Development, Web/App Dev, Testing/QA, Cloud Support, Data Analytics, or Cybersecurity. Can be followed by Online MCA later.
- **Online BBA (3 Yrs, UGC-DEB, ~₹15k–25k/sem):** Best for moving from technical floor work into Operations, Supply Chain, Procurement, Sales Engineering, HR, Finance, or running own business. Great launchpad for Online MBA.
- **Online B.Com / Online BA (3 Yrs, UGC-DEB, ~₹15k–25k/sem):** Most affordable, flexible 100% online graduation for SSC CGL, Banking, Railways non-tech, UPSC, or State PCS eligibility.
- **Partner Universities (via College Vidya):** Top NAAC A+/A++ online universities (Manipal, Amity, Uttaranchal, etc.) — 100% online classes & online exams.
